Registration marks

The design system draws “+” corner marks on every framed object. The school pages turn them off (the design files do the same with .blueprint > .corner { display:none }), so they live behind .show-marks — add the class, or flip the rule in components.css, to bring the drafting-sheet treatment back site-wide.
