1. About This Policy

Purba Barasat Adarsha Bidyapith respects the privacy of students, parents/guardians, staff and visitors. This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website, school management portal and online payment facility, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have.

It applies to: our website; online admission and enquiry forms; the parent/student portal; online fee payment; and communications by e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp or app notifications.

For personal data you give us, the School is the "Data Fiduciary" (the party that decides why and how personal data is used).

2. Information We Collect

a) Student information: name, date of birth, gender, photograph, admission/roll number, class and section, previous school records, attendance, academic results and progress reports, co-curricular participation, transport details, and relevant medical or special-needs information that you choose to share for the student's welfare.

b) Parent/guardian information: name, relationship, address, phone numbers, e-mail address, occupation (if asked at admission), and identity or address proof where required for admission or legal compliance.

c) Payment information: fee amount, date, transaction ID, payment status and mode (for example "UPI" or "card"). We do not collect or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or OTP. These are entered on the secure page of our Payment Gateway and are processed by it and your bank.

d) Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and basic log data, collected through server logs and cookies.

e) Communications: messages, enquiries, complaints, feedback and any documents you upload.

f) Photos and videos: images from school events, sports and activities (see Section 8).

3. Why We Use Your Information

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

  • admission processing and maintaining student records;
  • teaching, assessment, attendance, examinations and results;
  • student safety, health and welfare;
  • collecting fees, issuing receipts, processing refunds and preventing payment fraud;
  • communicating with parents/guardians about progress, events, circulars, holidays and emergencies;
  • operating and securing the website and portal;
  • meeting legal, regulatory, Board and audit requirements;
  • responding to enquiries and grievances.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use students' data for advertising, marketing to children or behavioural tracking.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

We process personal data (a) with your consent, given when you submit forms, register on the portal or use the payment facility; and (b) where the law allows or requires processing without consent, for example to comply with legal obligations, to respond to a medical emergency, or for functions required by an education authority.

Where you are giving consent for a student, you confirm that you are the student's parent or lawful guardian. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 11); withdrawal may affect our ability to provide certain services (for example, the portal or online payment).

5. Children's Data

Most of the personal data we hold relates to students who are under 18. Accordingly:

  • We collect and use a child's data only after obtaining the verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian, which is obtained through the admission form, portal registration or other consent forms.
  • We do not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • We use children's data only for the educational, safety and administrative purposes listed in Section 3.
  • Our public website is directed at adults. Children should use the portal only under a parent's or guardian's supervision.

6. Who We Share Information With

We share personal data only where necessary, and only with:

  • Payment Gateway and banks to process fee payments and refunds;
  • Service providers that host our systems or send SMS/e-mail/notifications on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations;
  • Examination boards, education authorities and government bodies where required for affiliation, registration, scholarships or compliance;
  • Courts, police or regulators where required by law or to protect safety;
  • Auditors and professional advisers under confidentiality.

We do not share data with third parties for their own marketing. If we ever need to transfer data outside India, we will do so only as permitted by Indian law.

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies (for login sessions and security) and may use basic analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the portal from working. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Photographs and Videos

Photographs and videos of students taken at school events may be used in newsletters, on the School's website, notice boards or official social media pages to showcase school life. We will seek separate consent for use of identifiable images of students in promotional material. You may withdraw consent or ask us to remove an image by writing to pbab.1963@gmail.com.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including HTTPS encryption, password-protected accounts, role-based access (staff see only what their role needs), regular backups and secure hosting. Online payments are processed on the Payment Gateway's secure systems.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law. Please keep your portal password confidential and tell us immediately if you suspect misuse.

10. How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in Section 3 or as required by law:

DataTypical retention
Student academic and admission recordsPermanently or as required by Board/education rules (for example transfer certificate and mark records)
Fee and payment recordsAs long as accounting, audit and tax law require
Website/log dataUp to 12 months
Enquiry forms from non-admitted applicantsUp to 12 months

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you (and the parent/guardian on behalf of a child) may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold and how it is used;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, and updating of data;
  • request erasure of data that is no longer needed for the purpose or legal requirement;
  • withdraw consent for processing that depends on consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • use our grievance process (Section 12) for any complaint about how your data is handled.

To exercise these rights, write to pbab.1963@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity (and, for a child, that you are the parent/guardian) and will respond within 30 days. Some records (for example academic records and accounts) must be retained under law even if you ask for deletion.
