About the school

Purba Barasat Adarsha Bidyapith stands in a pleasant setting alongside Taki Road, about one kilometre east of the Champadali Crossing in Barasat town. The school was established in 1963, inspired by the dedicated efforts of education enthusiasts from Kalikapur and the neighbouring areas.

Through the vision of those local patrons of education and the teaching staff, the original tiled-roof building housing just two classes — Class V and Class VI — has grown step by step into a five-storey, multipurpose Higher Secondary school teaching Science, Commerce and Arts.

Those who inaugurated the school by sounding its very first bell, and all who remain associated with it today — giving themselves directly and indirectly to its teaching and its growth — deserve heartfelt congratulations. Those who gave generously in many ways and are no longer with us remain fondly remembered.

Recognition

The school received government recognition in its first phase in 1965, for Classes V and VI, and in 1974 for Classes VII to X. With the approval of the West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education it was recognised in 1989 as a Higher Secondary school offering the Arts and Science streams; the Commerce stream was approved from the 1989–90 academic session.

Vocational wing

In August 2007 a vocational wing was introduced, affiliated to the West Bengal State Council of Vocational Education and Training. Well-equipped laboratories for these disciplines have been built with government financial assistance. The wing offers training in:

  • Computer Software and Computer Hardware
  • Information Technology
  • Telephone and Mobile Repairing
  • Paramedical and Health Care

Results and standing

Despite a very high student enrolment and the constraints of its infrastructure, the school's results in both the Madhyamik and the Higher Secondary examinations have been notably commendable, and it has been officially designated an ‘A’ Category school.
